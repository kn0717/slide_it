Slide It !!

A little cube, a sheet of graph paper, and whatever line you're brave enough to draw for it.

How to play

  1. 1Type an equation. A line like −0.5x + 3, or a parabola like 0.2x2 − 3 to send the cube up a hill.
  2. 2Press Start. The cube drops onto your line and slides toward the portal, uphill or downhill.
  3. 3Your line has to pass under the cube and through the portal. Miss, and you get to try the same level again.
  4. 4Drag the graph to look around. Reset restarts the level.
